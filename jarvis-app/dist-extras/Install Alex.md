# Installing Alex

Alex is your study partner — screenshot a problem and get the answer, or have it
build you a practice test with answers it's double-checked.

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the **Alex** disk image (the `.dmg` you got).
2. Drag **Alex** into the **Applications** folder shown.
3. Open **Applications** and double-click **Alex**.

## If macOS says *"Alex can't be opened"*

That just means Alex isn't signed by Apple yet — it's safe, it's the same app.
Two ways to open it:

**Easiest:** double-click **"Unlock Alex.command"** (it came with Alex), wait for
the ✅, then open Alex normally.

**Or, by hand:**
1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll down — you'll see *"Alex was blocked"*. Click **Open Anyway**.
3. Confirm **Open**. You only do this once.

*(On older macOS: right-click Alex → **Open** → **Open**.)*

## First launch

- Alex will ask for **Microphone** and **Screen Recording** permission. Screen
  Recording is what lets it read a problem off your screen — say yes.
- You start with a few **free credits**. When they run low, get a top-up code and
  type **`redeem YOUR-CODE`** in Alex.

## Using Alex

- Put a problem on your screen and ask **"what's the answer to this?"**
- **"make me a practice test on cell division"** → a real quiz with a verified key.
- **"how many credits do I have"** → check your balance anytime.

That's it. Happy studying.
